A little note from us

Garba is better when everyone feels safe.

We are so glad you are here. We introduce people, but you decide who to trust and when. We warmly encourage every match to talk on Instagram, or any other channel you are comfortable with, for a while before meeting in person.

  • Chat first. A few days of messages, or a quick video call, goes a long way.
  • Meet in public. Choose a busy, well-lit place, and go with friends or as a group if you can.
  • Trust your gut. You can say no, change plans or leave at any time, with no explanation needed.
  • Tell us. If anyone makes you uncomfortable, message us on Instagram. In an emergency, call 112 (women's helpline: 1091).

Terms & Safety

The fine print, in plain words.

By signing up, or by opening your matches, you confirm that you are 18 or older and agree to the points below. Please read them. They are here to look after you, and us.

Read the full terms

Version of 13 October 2026

1. What this is

The Garba Corridor, presented by Unrest ("we", "us"), is a free service that introduces Delhi University students who would like to go to a garba night together. We do not organise, host, sponsor, supervise or control any garba night, or any meeting between people who were introduced through this service.

2. Who can use it

You must be a current Delhi University student and at least 18 years old. You must give us true information, and use only your own Instagram ID.

3. We do not verify people

Names, colleges, years, Instagram IDs and everything else are provided by the people who sign up. We do not run background checks and we do not confirm anyone's identity, age, character or intentions. A match is an introduction, not a recommendation, endorsement or promise of safety.

4. Your choices are your own

You decide whether to say yes or no, whether to talk to a match, and whether to meet in person. We strongly encourage you to chat on Instagram or another channel first and to meet in public places, as set out in our safety note. Any conversation or meeting is between you and the other person, and you take part at your own risk.

5. Be kind, be real

Treat everyone with respect. Do not harass, pressure, threaten, impersonate or deceive anyone, and do not share another person's details or screenshots outside the purpose of getting to know each other. We may remove anyone, stop showing matches, or end the service at any time, without notice.

6. Your details

We collect your name, college, year and Instagram ID, and a PIN generated when you sign up. We use them to run the service and to contact you on Instagram. Every girl who signs up can see the name, college and Instagram ID of the boys who signed up, so she can say yes or no. A girl's details are shown to a boy only after she says yes. Keep your PIN private. We do not sell your details. They are stored using Google tools such as Sheets and Forms, which are run by Google. We aim to handle your information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can ask us to delete your details at any time by messaging us on Instagram.

7. No promises about the service

The service is provided "as is". We do not promise that you will get a match, that the site will always work, or that any match or meeting will turn out well.

8. Limits on our responsibility

To the fullest extent permitted by law, we, Unrest, and our founders, team members and volunteers are not responsible for any loss, injury, harm, cost or damage that arises from your use of this service, from anything another user says or does, or from any meeting or event you attend. Nothing in these terms limits any responsibility that cannot be limited under applicable law.

9. Reporting a problem

If anyone makes you feel unsafe or uncomfortable, message us on Instagram and we will act on it as best we can, including by removing the person. In an emergency, call 112 first. We may share information with the police or other authorities when the law requires it or when someone's safety is at risk.

10. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms, and the version date above will change when we do. These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts in Delhi will have jurisdiction.
